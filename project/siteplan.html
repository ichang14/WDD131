Oruguitas Deco Website Plan

Site Name

Oruguitas Deco

The name represents a personalized gift business that creates custom products such as hoodies, shirts, planners, mugs, pens, and other personalized gifts.

Site Purpose

The website will promote Oruguitas Deco and showcase its personalized products. Visitors will be able to browse products, view examples of previous work, learn about the company, and contact the business to place custom orders.

Scenarios

  • What personalized products does Oruguitas Deco offer?
  • How can I place a custom order?
  • How long does it take to complete an order?
  • Where can I contact Oruguitas Deco?

Color Schema

Primary Color - Pastel Purple (#8B83E6)

Used for headings, navigation menu, buttons, and logo accents.

Secondary Color - Pastel Pink (#D996C8)

Used for highlights, borders, call-to-action areas, and accents.

Neutral Colors

White (#FFFFFF) for backgrounds and Dark Gray (#333333) for body text.

Typography

Poppins

Used for headings, navigation menus, and buttons.

Open Sans

Used for paragraphs, descriptions, and general content.
